# 示例 Example：新工作三个月，想法总被否定 / Three months into a new job, ideas keep getting shut down

## 用户输入 / User input

> 我入职新公司三个月了。我有不少改进流程的想法，但主管很强势，每次提出来都被否定。我是该继续争取，还是先忍着？
>
> I joined a new company three months ago. I have plenty of ideas for improving our processes, but my manager is very assertive and shoots them down every time. Should I keep pushing, or hold back for now?

## 解读 / Reading

**【处境 Situation】**
你是新人，有能力也有想法；上面是一位强势、暂时不愿改变的主管。你在犹豫要不要继续推。
You are new, capable and full of ideas; above you is a strong manager who is not ready to change. You are unsure whether to keep pushing.

**【本卦 Hexagram】大畜 · 山天大畜（111001）**
内卦乾：你有实力、想开创；外卦艮：环境像一座山，把你挡住。乾被艮所止，正是"蓄积"之象——力量不是没用，而是被要求先储存起来。
Inner Qian: you are strong and want to act. Outer Gen: the environment is a mountain in your way. Heaven held inside the mountain is the image of accumulation — your strength is not wasted, it is being stored.
备选：小畜（111011），如果主管不是"挡住"而是"慢慢磨"，差别在于阻力的硬度。
Alternative: Xiaoxu (111011), if the manager is wearing you down gently rather than blocking you outright.

**【卦辞 Judgement】**「利贞，不家食吉，利涉大川。」
守正有利；不在家里白吃饭（凭本事做事）则吉；适合去做大事。
Perseverance is favourable; not eating at home (earning your place by real work) brings good fortune; it is favourable to cross the great river.

**【爻位 Line】初九：「有厉，利已。」**
入职三个月，处在最下面的初爻。爻辞直说：此时硬冲有危险，停下来有利。
Three months in, you are at the bottom line. The text is blunt: pushing now is dangerous; stopping is favourable.

**【四个角度 Four angles】**
- 综卦 无妄 Reverse, Wuwang：从主管的角度看，他要的是"不出乱子"、按规矩来；你的想法在他眼里可能是"妄动"。 / From the manager's side, he wants no surprises; your ideas may look reckless to him.
- 错卦 萃 Inverse, Cui：你缺的是"聚"——同事的支持和共识。 / What you lack is gathering: allies and shared support.
- 互卦 归妹 Nuclear, Guimei：内部隐含"不对等关系"，你现在处在被动的位置，先认清这一点。 / Underneath lies an unequal relationship; accept that you are in the weaker position for now.
- 之卦 蛊 Changed, Gu：如果这一阶段发生变化，局面会走向"有积弊需要整顿"——那时你积累的想法才会被需要。 / If this stage shifts, it heads toward Gu, a mess that needs repair — which is when your stored ideas will be wanted.

**【曾仕强怎么说 What Zeng says】**
大畜是"蓄德日新"：时机未到时，积累比展示更重要；审时而动，慎始敬终。
Daxu is about accumulating and renewing your virtue daily: when the time is not right, building up matters more than showing off; act when the time comes, careful from start to finish.

**【下一步 Next steps】**
- 该做 Do now：把每个想法写成一页纸（问题、方案、成本、风险），先存起来；在本职工作上做出无可挑剔的成绩（"不家食吉"）；私下找一两位资深同事交流，积累支持（补"萃"）。 / Write each idea up on one page (problem, fix, cost, risk) and keep it; make your core work flawless; talk privately with one or two senior colleagues to build support.
- 不该做 Avoid：在会上反复当众推方案；越过主管去找更高层。 / Don't keep pushing proposals in meetings; don't go over the manager's head.
- 留意信号 Watch for：出现故障、客户投诉、主管主动问"有没有更好的办法"——这就是"蛊"的时机，拿出你存好的方案。 / A failure, a customer complaint, or the manager asking "is there a better way?" — that is the Gu moment to bring out your prepared plan.

这只是帮助思考的视角，不是预言；做决定的是你自己。
This is a lens for thinking, not a prediction; the decision is yours.
