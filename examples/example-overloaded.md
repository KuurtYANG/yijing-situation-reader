# 示例 Example：老板不断加任务又抱怨没做完 / A boss who keeps adding tasks, then complains they aren't done

## 用户输入 / User input

> 我老板一直给我派任务，我已经超负荷了，他还抱怨为什么工作没做完。
>
> My boss keeps giving me tasks and I'm overloaded, then he complains why the jobs aren't done yet.

## 解读 / Reading

**【处境 Situation】**
老板不断给你加任务，你已经超负荷，他却还抱怨工作没做完。你被夹在"接不完"和"被指责"之间。
Your boss keeps piling on tasks. You're already overloaded, and he complains that things aren't finished. You're caught between more than you can handle and being blamed for it.

**【本卦 Hexagram】大过 · 泽风大过（011110）**
内卦是巽，代表你自己：柔顺，什么都接下来。外卦是兑，代表环境：口舌，也就是老板的抱怨。中间四个阳爻挤在一起，两端各只有一个阴爻，像一根房梁中段压得太重、两头撑不住。
The inner trigram, Xun, is you: yielding, saying yes to everything. The outer trigram, Dui, is the setting: talk, here the boss's complaints. Four solid lines are crowded into the middle, with one broken line at each end, like a roof beam overloaded in the middle with weak supports at both ends.
备选：困卦（010110）。如果重点不是"太多"，而是"怎么解释他都不信"，就更接近困卦。
Alternative: Kun, Oppression (010110). It fits better if the core problem is less the volume and more that nothing you say gets believed.

**【卦辞 Judgement】「栋桡，利有攸往，亨。」**
房梁已经压弯了，但"利有攸往"：主动采取行动才会通达，一味硬扛不行。
The ridgepole sags, yet "it is favourable to have somewhere to go": things work out only if you act. Silently enduring won't.

**【爻位 Line】九三：「栋桡，凶。」**
你处在第三爻：执行层，上面压下来，下面没人分担，是最危险的位置。曾仕强讲这一爻说"你会发现你过分地刚"：九三的问题是独自硬撑，不肯向旁边求助。
You're at the third line: the person who executes, with pressure from above and no one below to share it. Zeng's comment on this line is "you will find you are too rigid". Line three's problem is carrying everything alone and not asking for support.

**【四个角度 Four angles】**
- 综卦 Reverse，还是大过：老板很可能也在承受上面的压力，他的抱怨未必是针对你。 / Still Daguo: your boss is likely under the same load from above; his complaints may be pressure passed down.
- 错卦 Inverse，颐卦：你缺的是先确定自己真正能承担多少。 / Yi, Nourishment: what's missing is knowing how much you can sustainably take on.
- 互卦 Nuclear，乾卦：内部硬碰硬，你闷头硬扛，他强推任务。 / Qian: force meeting force, you grimly carrying, him pushing.
- 之卦 Changed，困卦：拖下去会人被耗尽，说什么都没人信。 / Kun, Oppression: drag this out and you're worn out, with nothing you say believed.

**【曾仕强怎么说 What Zeng says】**
大过的核心是"刚柔相济方能化险"。九三独自硬撑，所以"栋桡，凶"；九四得到下面的支撑，所以"栋隆，吉"。区别在于有没有找到支撑。
The heart of Daguo is that only firmness balanced with flexibility gets you through danger. Line three carries the load alone and the beam sags; line four gets support from below and the beam stands straight. The difference is whether you have support.

**【下一步 Next steps】**
- 该做 Do now：
  1. 列一张任务清单，写明时间和截止日，拿给老板看。 / Write one list of tasks with the time each needs and its deadline, and show it to him.
  2. 请他排优先级："这周我能完成这三项，其余哪些可以延后？" / Ask him to rank priorities: "This week I can finish these three. Which of the others can wait?"
  3. 主动要支撑：人手、延期，或可以分出去的任务。 / Ask for support: an extra pair of hands, an extension, or tasks that can be handed off.
  4. 每周简短主动汇报进度。 / Give a short progress update every week.
- 不该做 Avoid：默默全部接下再靠加班硬撑；情绪化地顶回去或辩解。 / Taking everything quietly and working overtime to cover it; arguing back or defending yourself in frustration.
- 留意信号 Watch for：他开始问"哪些可以放一放"，局面在好转；清单摆出来后他照样加任务、照样指责，就已进入困卦，要做好书面记录，必要时考虑换个环境。 / If he starts asking "what can we drop?", things are improving. If he still adds tasks and still blames you after seeing the list, you're in Oppression: keep written records and consider whether to move on.

这是帮助思考的视角，不是预言；怎么做由你决定。
This is a lens for thinking, not a prediction; the decision is yours.
